Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Anna VetoLayer handles information when it evaluates proposed AI-agent actions and, when enabled, coordinates human review.

Information processed

The app processes the action details you provide, deterministic policy findings, contextual-analysis results, reviewer evidence and rationale, and the resulting Decision Receipt. Do not include credentials, raw secrets, or personal data that is not necessary for the review.

The optional shared-review service also processes Clerk user identifiers, team membership and roles, assignments, comments, decision history, and notification records. Clerk handles sign-in credentials; Anna VetoLayer does not receive or store user passwords.

How information is used

Information is used only to evaluate actions, enforce policy and separation of duties, preserve an audit trail, deliver review outcomes, maintain security, and troubleshoot the service. Anna VetoLayer does not sell personal information or use Decision Receipt content for advertising.

Storage and service providers

  • Anna app storage keeps single-user Decision Receipts within the app's granted storage scope.
  • Neon stores optional shared-review teams, receipts, comments, events, and notifications.
  • Clerk authenticates users and supplies stable account identifiers.
  • Vercel hosts the reviewer portal and API and may retain operational request logs.

Each provider handles data under its own terms and privacy commitments.

Retention and deletion

Single-user receipts can be cleared from the app by the user. Shared-review teams default to a 365-day receipt retention setting and may configure a period from 30 to 2,555 days. Deleting a team cascades its queue, comments, events, and notifications. Some audit records may be retained when required for security, legal obligations, or an active incident hold.

Security and your choices

Shared-review API access requires a valid Clerk session and team membership. Roles, self-approval controls, and atomic decisions limit unauthorized or conflicting resolutions. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

You may choose not to enable shared review, avoid submitting unnecessary information, export your receipts, or clear local receipts. For access or deletion requests involving a shared team, contact the team administrator first.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, use the project's support channel. Do not include secrets or sensitive receipt content in a public issue; request a private follow-up instead.

Changes

Material changes will be reflected on this page with a new “last updated” date. Continued use after a change means the updated policy applies to later processing.

Anna VetoLayer evaluates and records proposed actions. It does not execute the underlying third-party action.
