CONTROL PATH

A clear path from intent to accountable decision.

  1. 01

    Propose

    An agent or operator submits a structured description of the consequential action.

  2. 02

    Evaluate

    The policy engine checks hard rules, risk signals, permissions, and required evidence.

  3. 03

    Resolve

    Ambiguous actions receive Anna context or a reviewer decision with rationale.

  4. 04

    Remember

    An integrity-tagged Decision Receipt preserves the decision and its lineage.

NON-NEGOTIABLES

Some decisions should never become a negotiation.

Hard policy blocks are authoritative. Missing critical evidence cannot become an AI allow. Evaluation failures fail closed.
